Services

What weshoot

Everything below happens either in the studio on Commercial Street or wherever you need us — our spacious studio is home, but home and office calls are part of the service.

01 — Studio portraits

Portraits, done with intent

Backdrops, studio lighting, and direction if posing isn't your natural talent. Solo portraits, couples, siblings, birthday sets and creative concept shoots — the paper-burst and coloured-gel work you'll see in the gallery is all ours.

Editing is included, and it's the thing reviewers call out most often.

02 — Weddings & harusi

Your day, start to finish

Preparations, ceremony, the family and bridal party portraits, then the reception. Photography and filming from the same team, so nothing falls between two suppliers.

Wedding work lives on its own feed at @emage_weddings. Message us with your date early — Saturdays in December go first.

Check availability

03 — Family, kids & maternity

The ones you'll actually print

Family sittings, birthday sets, maternity and newborn. Children set their own pace and we work around it rather than against it — more than one review mentions how good the children's photos come out.

04 — Corporate & graduation

Professional, and quick about it

Headshots for LinkedIn and company profiles, full-length professional portraits, team and staff photos. We'll set up at your office if bringing everyone to Thika town isn't practical.

Graduation gown portraits too — in the studio or at the ceremony, turned around while the day still means something.

05 — Also on the counter

The everyday things

Instant passport photos

Walk in, walk out with them. Correct sizing for passports, visas and ID applications.

Event coverage

Corporate functions, launches, celebrations, ruracio and send-offs. Stills, video, or both.

Video & filming

We're a filming establishment as much as a photo studio — highlight films, promos and event video.

Home & office calls

The studio is our base, not our limit. Tell us where and we'll bring the kit to you.

Framing & prints

Capture, frame, deliver. Ask about print sizes and framing when you book.

Not sure what you need?
